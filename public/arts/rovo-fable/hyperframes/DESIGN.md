## Style Prompt

Rovo Fable is an archival glyph reel: a white rounded hexagonal symbol keeps returning as botanical specimens, maps, scientific scans, stamps, sheet music, and lab fragments. The canvas feels like museum evidence under a flatbed scanner, with aged paper, cyanotype blue, field-note ink, muted biological reds and greens, and brief cross-dissolves that mimic rapid handmade frame assembly.

## Colors

- `#f1ead7` aged ivory paper background
- `#221b16` catalog ink and dark frame edges
- `#315f72` cyanotype and spectral blue
- `#7e3d31` stamped red and specimen marks
- `#5d6f3f` herbarium green

## Typography

- Display: `Georgia`, heavy serif for the art title
- Annotation: `IBM Plex Mono`, technical specimen labels and frame IDs

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use glossy 3D, neon, app mockups, or cinematic spotlights.
- Do not turn the symbol into a simple filled texture mask.
- Do not vary the symbol position across frames.
- Do not use hard jump cuts between archival frames.
